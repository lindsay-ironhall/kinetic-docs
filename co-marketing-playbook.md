# Co-Marketing Playbook

Kinetics' Marketing Playbook is here to help facilitate a successful and mutually beneficial partnership between us and you. Here you will find information about Kinetic, the Flare ecosystem, resources, and links.

## About Kinetic

Kinetic is a pioneering lending and borrowing protocol operating on the Flare Network. Led by a dedicated trio of core contributors, Kinetic has forged a strategic partnership with Rome Blockchain Labs, leveraging their robust technical infrastructure for uninterrupted communication and security. With a focus on harnessing the expertise gained from years in the decentralized lending sector, Kinetic aims to cater to users seeking advanced strategies and reliable decentralized data facilitated by FTSO, LayerCake, and FAssets on the Flare Network. Its mission is to establish a liquidity hub enabling both individuals and institutions to maximize the potential of Flare, fostering DeFi expansion.
